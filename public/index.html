ⓘ Operational Metric Definitions & Formulas

Zero-Denominator Rule: Whenever the total denominator (e.g. eligible endings or scheduled classes) is 0 for a given period, the system displays N/A rather than 0% to preserve statistical accuracy.

🔄 Renewal Rate

(Renewed Packages / Total Eligible Package Endings in Period) × 100

Measures percentage of students whose packages ended in the selected period who renewed their enrollment.

⚠️ Churn Rate

(Explicitly Churned Packages / Total Eligible Package Endings in Period) × 100

Measures percentage of ending packages marked as explicitly churned. Unconfirmed/pending endings are kept as pending and do not distort churn.

⚡ Class Delivery Rate

(Conducted & SSC-Verified Classes / Total Scheduled Classes) × 100

Percentage of scheduled academic sessions successfully delivered and verified by the SSC team.

📅 Rescheduling Rate

(Unique Classes Rescheduled / Total Scheduled Classes) × 100

Measures frequency of class schedule changes. Grouped by original class instance to prevent duplicate counting.

✅ Verification Rate

(SSC Verified Wrap-up Reports / Total Conducted Faculty Session Reports) × 100

Indicates SSC responsiveness in reviewing and finalizing completed faculty class reports.

👨‍🎓 Attendance Rate

(Present Classes / Total Student Class Occurrences) × 100

Student class attendance percentage across all scheduled session occurrences.
